Our story

Big Ideas Built With Little Habits.

Making the invisible internet visible, for every generation.

Meet our approach →

Built for our family. Shared with yours.

After years auditing internet systems, Shailin Dhar became a father and began asking a different question: how would his own family learn to use them?

Working with leading child development specialists, he began turning complex systems into stories, toys and everyday habits. Every product and lesson in our curriculum is developed with them.
