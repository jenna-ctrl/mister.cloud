The big little idea

One everyday action can become a data record.

1

What happened?

A door opened. A show played. A speaker heard a wake word. Start with the event.

2

Who is it linked to?

The device may know an account or profile. That does not tell us who was there.

3

Where did it go?

Did it stay, travel to a service, or get saved? Check and mark the rest unknown.

Four useful distinctions

Keep the story honest.

Small differences help families make better choices without jumping from a clue to a conclusion.

1

Recorded ≠ sent

A device can make a local record without sending it over the internet.

2

Sent ≠ kept forever

Transmission and storage are different questions. Ask where it went and how long it remains.

3

Account ≠ person

A child may watch through a grandparent’s account. The account name is not proof of who watched.

4

Guess ≠ observation

“Someone is home” may be an inference from a sensor, not something it directly saw.

Take the habit with you

Three questions for a shared-device home.

Whose profile is this?

On a TV or tablet, check which account and protections are active for the current viewer.

What starts the listening?

With a speaker, learn what triggers it, what is processed, and where the settings live.

Can we check the route?

Find privacy controls and retention information. A connection alone does not reveal contents.
