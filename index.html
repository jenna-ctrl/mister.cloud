Why families trust Mister Cloud

Built by the people who audit the internet — for their own kids first

Mister Cloud was created by a big-tech auditor who has invented patented bot-detection technology since 2017 — the systems that tell real humans apart from automated fraud at internet scale. For years, that deep systems knowledge and investigative experience quietly kept his own family and immediate circle safer online.

His wife, a neuropsychology and child-development expert, helped him turn it into something a child could actually hold: a catalogue of curriculum, books, and toys that taught the children across their family how to be safe online — in language a toddler, a teen, and a grandparent could each understand.

What they built to protect their own family is now shared with yours.

We turn invisible data centers and subsea cables into familiar stories, teaching families the foundational habits of digital life — starting with pause before you share.

Our Mission

To teach data privacy, consent, and safe device habits through books, toys, and zero-data apps before screen habits harden. We aim to move digital safety education earlier into a child’s life through simple recognition and repetition.

Screens are everywhere, so our goal is to empower families to live with connected devices safely and without fear — building a population of internet users that do not reflexively share data with “the cloud.”

Our little ones

Children are on connected screens before they can talk, walk, read, or type. A toddler can summon the cloud with a single tap. We give them their first, gentle framework for consent and privacy, built around one memorable boundary: “No thank you, Mister Cloud.”

💛

Our elders

Grandparents face calls, texts, and emails engineered to exploit their love and their fears — the “your grandchild is in trouble” call, the “you owe back taxes” threat. Grandparent Mode gives them the context and the calm to recognize a scam before it works.

The invisible internet is a physical web.
We give you the map so you don’t feel lost.

Most people are never taught the basics: where their phone or tablet fits into the larger internet, and the order of operations by which a message actually travels from one place to another.

That single understanding — that the internet has a physical body, and information moves through it in a predictable order — transforms how a person navigates the web. A suspicious text becomes easier to question. An urgent pop-up loses its power. Sharing becomes a choice, not a reflex.

Meet the family: the four skies

Real clouds sort themselves by altitude and shape — and so does the internet. Each member of the Mister Cloud family makes one invisible layer easy to picture.

Cumi

Local Tier · 0–2 km

Your own devices & local storage — the files right here on your phone, tablet, and hard drive.

Alto

Network Tier · 2–7 km

Cloud syncing & collaboration — the shared drives that patch everyone’s copies together.

Ciri

Global Tier · 7 km+

High-speed internet & global streams — fiber, 5G, and the data racing around the world.

Nimbus

Processing Tier · the giants

Big data, AI & cybersecurity — the server farms that crunch, decide, and defend.
